Game idea:
	Trampoline platformer to get to a goal (prize?)
	Trampoline platforms are flowers or lilypads, they make you jump higher the more items coins you collect
	Setting is in a jungle or forest and goes into space as you get higher
	There are walls that block your way and you need to level up your jump power to be able to break them.
	As you progres in the game the obstacles get harder and the walls are more strong.
